About Heartpenned

You know that feeling. Standing in the card aisle, reading one after another, shaking your head.

Some say too much. Some don't say nearly enough. Some are close — but not quite right. Not quite you.

Maybe the card is too grand for what you actually have. Maybe it's not grand enough. Maybe you love this person in a way that's specific and deep and completely yours — and nothing on that rack comes close to capturing it.

So you settle. You sign your name and hope it lands.

It doesn't have to be that way.

The truth is, you already know exactly what to say. You know the specific thing they do that nobody else notices. You remember the moment that changed things between you. You know how this relationship actually feels.

You just need help finding the words.

That's what we built. Answer a few honest questions and we'll help you write something personal, specific, and true. The kind of card people keep.

Real relationships deserve the right words.
For those the card aisle was not built for.
