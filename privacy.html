Privacy at HeartPenned

HeartPenned helps you write greeting cards. Here's what happens to what you share.

What you type. Your answers (names, the occasion, details about the person) are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, only to write your card. We don't use them to build a profile of you.

Cards you send. If you email a card or create a share link, we keep the card for 90 days so the link works. Email is delivered through our email provider, Resend. If someone asks not to receive cards from HeartPenned, we keep their email address so we don't send to them again.

Reminders. If you set a reminder, we keep your email address and the dates so we can send it. You can unsubscribe from any reminder email.

Testers and study participants. If you're testing HeartPenned or taking part in a study, we keep the finished card text and your ratings, your answers to the study questions and which send options you tried, for up to 12 months to improve the cards. They're linked only to a tester ID, never your name. To have yours deleted, contact us.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics to see how people use the site, like which pages they visit. It isn't used for study participants.

Feedback forms. Some testers are shown a short feedback form run by Tally.

Where it's kept. The site runs on Vercel, and data is stored with Upstash.
